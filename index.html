Nookery Privacy Policy

Nookery Publishing, a DBA of HappyChaos LLC, Texas, USA.
Effective October 18, 2026. Website: nookery.net

This policy covers "Nookery Publishing Research," a private research script that uses the Pinterest API. It is used only by the owner of Nookery Publishing. It has no public users, no sign-up and no accounts for other people.

What the script does

The script reads public, aggregate trend information from Pinterest, such as which search terms and topics are growing. The owner uses this to decide which books to publish. The script is read-only. It does not post, pin, comment, follow, message or change anything on Pinterest.

What data we collect

  • We do not collect personal information about Pinterest users.
  • We do not read other people's Pins, Boards or profiles.
  • We store only trend terms and their counts or growth figures.
  • The owner's own Pinterest authorization token is stored in a private configuration file on the owner's computer.

How we use data

Trend data is used only for internal market research and product planning at Nookery Publishing. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or to contact anyone.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose Pinterest data to anyone. We do not combine it with personal information from other sources.

Storage, security and retention

Data is kept on the owner's own devices and is not published. We keep trend data for as long as it is useful for research and delete it on request or when it is no longer needed. If the Pinterest authorization is revoked, the script stops working and the stored token is deleted.

Children

The script does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the script's use of data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through nookery.net.
