Nookery Privacy Policy

Nookery Publishing, a DBA of HappyChaos LLC, Texas, USA.
Effective October 18, 2026. Website: nookery.net

This policy covers "Nookery Publishing Research," a private script that uses the Pinterest API. It is used only by the owner of Nookery Publishing. It has no public users, no sign-up and no accounts for other people.

What the script does

  • Reads public, aggregate trend information from Pinterest, such as which search terms and topics are growing, to help the owner decide which books to publish.
  • Creates and schedules the owner's own Pins to promote the owner's books.
  • Reads and manages the owner's own Pinterest ad campaigns and reads their performance reports.

The script acts only on the owner's own Pinterest account and ad account. It does not follow, message, comment on or change anything belonging to other Pinterest users.

What data we collect

  • We do not collect personal information about Pinterest users.
  • We do not read other people's Pins, Boards or profiles.
  • We store trend terms with their counts or growth figures, and the owner's own Pin, ad and reporting data.
  • The owner's own Pinterest authorization token is stored in a private configuration file on the owner's computer.

How we use data

Data is used only for internal market research, marketing and product planning at Nookery Publishing. We do not use it for profiling or to contact anyone.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose Pinterest data to anyone. We do not combine it with personal information from other sources.

Storage, security and retention

Data is kept on the owner's own devices and is not published. We keep data for as long as it is useful for research and delete it on request or when it is no longer needed. If the Pinterest authorization is revoked, the script stops working and the stored token is deleted.

Children

The script does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the script's use of data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through nookery.net.
